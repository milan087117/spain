About Spain

Spain is a beautiful country in Southern Europe. It is famous for sunny weather, delicious food, beaches, football, and friendly people.

Barcelona

Weather: Warm summers and mild winters.

Tradition: Famous for flamenco music, football, and local festivals.

Restaurants: Spain has tapas bars, seafood restaurants, and local cafes.

Safety: Barcelona is mostly safe, but tourists should watch their bags in busy places.


Madrid

Weather: Hot summers and cool winters.

Tradition: Madrid has traditional Spanish dances and food markets.

Restaurants: Spain has tapas bars, seafood restaurants, and local cafes.

Safety: Madrid is a safe city with good public transport and many police officers.


Seville

Weather: Very hot summers and short winters.

Tradition: Seville is famous for flamenco and colorful street festivals.

Restaurants: Spain has tapas bars, seafood restaurants, and local cafes.

Safety: Seville is calm and friendly, but tourists should stay careful at night.

Events

La Tomatina

This is a famous tomato festival in summer. People throw tomatoes and have fun together.

San Fermin

This festival is famous for the running of the bulls. Many tourists visit every year.

Music Festivals

Spain has many summer music festivals near beaches and big cities.
